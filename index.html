Service Design

An app designed to make hospital waiting more transparent, informed and reassuring. Built through design thinking and patient journey research, it uses real-time queue updates and multilingual accessibility to reduce uncertainty and communication gaps.

Sutra Edit · Fashion intelligence

Startup Pitch

The business intelligence behind India's next generation of fashion brands. An India-focused platform that turns fragmented industry information into sharper decisions for emerging fashion founders.

Three layers: intelligence (weekly market edits and pricing benchmarks), community (founder-to-founder learning) and consulting (positioning, channels and growth), sold through a tiered yearly membership.

The live website

Sutra Edit, online

The concept is built out as a working website: "India's fashion founders build here." It carries an editorial insights desk with monthly issues, the platform and founder circle, consulting, and a weekly newsletter sign-up.

Spatial design · Concept to space

Spatial Design Reimagined

A serene Mediterranean-style outdoor café courtyard, redesigned from concept to space. Terracotta roof tiles, white stucco walls, blue arched windows and a picket-fence patio make the compact espresso station feel open, calm and inviting.

The project takes the idea through isometric views, floor plans and elevations, then follows the customer flow through the courtyard. It ends with perspective renders of the space in use.
